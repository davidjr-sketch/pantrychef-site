PantryChef Support

Need help or want to report a problem? Email wrxirons@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, app version, and a description of what happened. Beta testers can also submit feedback in TestFlight.

Getting meal ideas

Search for pantry ingredients, add the ones you have, then tap Suggest Meals. Closest matches appear first. Water is assumed available, but other staples need to be selected. Pantry matches check ingredient names, so check quantities and preparation before cooking.

Favorites

Tap a recipe's heart to save it, then open Favorites. Ingredient availability reflects your current pantry.

Shopping list

In a recipe, tap Add missing ingredients. Choose a suitable alternative for an item when available. Mark purchased items and tap Add purchased items to pantry. Only marked purchases move into the pantry.

Your data

PantryChef stores pantry ingredients, favorites, and the shopping list on your device. The current app has no account or cloud sync. Your device backups may include this data. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Cooking guidance

Times are estimates. Ingredients listed as cooked need to be prepared first. Check allergies, package directions, and food-safety guidance before cooking. Please report recipe instructions that seem unclear or inaccurate.
